Jee to miss IEM Beijing due to visa issues

Community
25-10-2026 17:42

TYLOO will be without their primary AWPer at the upcoming event on home soil.

Dongkai "Jee" Ji will miss IEM Beijing due to visa issues, leaving TYLOO to field a stand-in for the tournament.

The Chinese AWPer has been unable to complete the necessary visa formalities in time to link up with his teammates ahead of the event. The issue concerns his travel arrangements prior to the tournament, rather than his eligibility to enter China, and will prevent him from returning to Beijing in time for the competition.

TYLOO are yet to name a replacement, with the team now facing a late reshuffle as preparations for the event enter their final stages. It remains unclear who will take over AWPing duties in Jee's absence.

The setback leaves the Chinese side without a key piece of their lineup for an event on home soil. Beyond finding a fifth player, the team will have to adjust their setups around the absence of their dedicated sniper, with little time to integrate a stand-in.

For Jee, the enforced absence means missing the opportunity to compete in front of a home crowd. No timeline has been set for the resolution of the visa issue, although the change is expected to be temporary.

TYLOO's final lineup for IEM Beijing will be confirmed once a replacement has been secured.
